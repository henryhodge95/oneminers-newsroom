New pillar · X + LinkedIn · proposal for Henry

OneMiners Newsroom

One newsroom, two editions. On X we run the wire: three or four short posts a day where the post itself is the news. On LinkedIn we publish a weekly paper: four Page posts, one newsletter edition, and daily posts from our own people. Both cover the Bitcoin-mining industry, its live debates and its events, alongside our own sites. It replaces most of what we post on both platforms today, so the daily volume doesn't grow.

What changed on the platforms this year

Checked 1 Oct 2026. Each claim carries its publish date, and the sources are listed at the bottom.

20 Jan · 1 OctXFor You is ranked by a Grok-based model. It rewards replies, quotes, DM shares and copied links (a copied link is worth about 40 likes). Mutes and reports are heavy negatives. Several posts in a row from one account are discounted, and posts older than 48 hours drop out. xai-org/x-algorithm, synced from production almost daily since Aug
13 Jul 2026XMutual follows are boosted in the feed and in replies. That makes a daily reply session with the big mining accounts a direct way to grow.
28 Jul 2026XX says there's no link penalty any more. Links still underperform: Buffer measured 2.25% median engagement on link posts against 3.56% for text (5 Mar 2026). Keep the source in a reply.
11 Apr · 16 JulXOriginality crackdown. Aggregator payouts were cut, duplicate-clip detection got 3× stronger, and asking for engagement over and over can remove an account from the creator programme. Original Content Rewards opened for applications on 8 Sep.
12 Jan 2026XArticles are open to every Premium tier. They show as a feed card with no reach boost, and ran at about 1.0× median in our sample. One a week is enough. We're at six in 10 days.
23 Apr · 1 JulXCommunities shut down in May. Live Studio for desktop streaming launched for Premium on 1 Jul. Custom Timelines (21 Apr) let people pin a topic, so posts that are clearly about mining get filed under mining.
12 Mar 2026LinkedInOne LLM now picks feed candidates by meaning, and a sequence model ranks them on each member's last 1,000+ interactions. Staying on one topic is what builds reach. Interest-based distribution has risen from 5% to 50% of the feed (van der Blom, 7 May).
21 May 2026LinkedInGeneric AI-written posts and automated comments are pushed out of recommendations. Vessel drafts for people's profiles must carry their own first-hand detail, or they'll be buried.
25 May · 2 SepLinkedInCadence: LinkedIn itself says 2–5 posts a week, two of them video. AuthoredUp finds engagement peaks at 4–5 a week, and van der Blom finds daily posting cuts reach per post by 26%. We post daily.
16 Mar · 2 SepLinkedInDocuments (PDF carousels) lead on reach and engagement: 1.39× reach on 3M posts. Video views are down 36% year on year, and clips under 30 s are the weakest. Polls get 1.78× reach but only 0.37× engagement.
~Mar · 27 AugLinkedInA Page newsletter notifies each subscriber once, on one channel, and only about 59% got the email in one measured case. There's no one-a-day cap; we were wrong about that on 29 Sep. A Page needs 150+ followers, and we have 2,268.
22 Jun · 23 JulLinkedInA live stream must now be attached to a scheduled LinkedIn Event and needs a third-party streaming tool. Collaborative posts (a Page plus up to 5 co-authors) are rolling out. A body link costs about 19% reach, and 0–3 hashtags beat 3–5 (van der Blom, 7 May).

The build, in order

1 · Desks on every draft

Day 1

A series field on drafts (wire, quote, data, debate, machine, sites, event, archive, edition). The Desk groups by it, the Calendar counts it per day, and the posting log inherits it from the posted link, so the Month page shows which desks earn their slot.

2 · Wire tab on the Desk

Days 1–3

Candidate stories from the radar, each pre-drafted by the vessel's hourly job with a JUST IN line, the source reply and a LinkedIn angle. New mining-native sources: The Energy Mag, Blockspace, the Hashrate Index weekly roundup, listed miners' production updates, and SEC filings for the listed miners. The Google News and CFTC feeds still refuse the Worker, so they move to the vessel.

3 · New card templates

Days 2–4

Added to the Worker's renderer (still no AI and no image credits): one-stat card, quote card, spec card (no price field), fleet card. LinkedIn documents are assembled as a PDF from slide cards, which closes the "LinkedIn PDF assembly is manual" gap.

4 · Data card goes weekly

Day 1

The 07:00 daily card moves to Monday, plus one on each difficulty adjustment. No measured account runs a daily data card. Hashrate Index's weekly one beats its median 2–3×.

5 · Industry events in Moments

Days 3–5

Moments only holds holidays today. It gains the conferences, earnings windows, difficulty adjustments and Core releases, each with a lead time. On event days the Desk switches to event mode: 5–8 X slots and a quick-post card for whoever is on the floor.

Week 2

Thursday: the vessel assembles The Mining Brief from what actually went out that week, plus the network week and one note from our sites. That gives a newsletter draft and an X Article draft. You approve on Friday morning.
